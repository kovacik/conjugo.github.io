<h2>Looking up or practising: which do you need?</h2>

<p>A conjugator answers "what is the <em>nous</em> form of <em>savoir</em> in the passé composé?" in a second. That solves the problem in front of you and teaches you almost nothing, because you never had to recall it.</p>

<p>Practice apps work the other way round. They ask the question and make you produce the answer, which is slower and more annoying, and is the only thing that moves a form from "I'd recognise it" to "it comes out on its own". If you write French, you want a conjugator. If you speak it, you want practice. Plenty of learners keep one of each.</p>
